Vision of the Faculty of Law

"To Realize a Faculty of Law That Excels in the Development of Legal Science for Society at Large, Inspired by Religiosity and Humanism."

Mission of the Faculty of Law

  • To deliver outstanding and impactful legal education for society at large, grounded in the values of Religiosity and Humanism.
  • To conduct legal research that is relevant to the development of technology and industry.
  • To carry out innovative and integrative community service activities that enhance legal knowledge and awareness in society.
  • To establish Tri Dharma Higher Education collaborations with various stakeholders both domestically and internationally.

Objectives of the Faculty of Law

  • To enhance the quality of legal education through innovative, adaptive, and outcome-oriented learning aligned with the needs of the workforce.
  • To improve the quality of research conducted by faculty and students that contributes to the advancement of legal science and provides solutions to legal issues in society.
  • To optimize community service through legal counseling, consultation, and legal aid that benefits society and supports national development.
  • To enhance human resource quality by encouraging lecturers to pursue further studies, advance academic positions, obtain professional certifications, and develop continuous competencies.
  • To expand national and international collaborations with universities, government institutions, the business sector, professional organizations, and law enforcement agencies to improve the quality of education, research, and community service.

Goals of the Faculty of Law

  • Achievement of a high-quality learning process through an Outcome-Based Education (OBE) curriculum, enhanced lecturer competence, and improved graduate achievement and competitiveness.
  • Increased quantity and quality of research by faculty and students, resulting in scientific publications, intellectual property, and policy recommendations in the field of law.
  • Improved quality and quantity of community service activities through legal counseling, legal consultation, legal aid, and community empowerment.
  • Realization of effective, efficient, transparent, and accountable faculty governance through strengthened quality assurance systems, improved human resource competencies, and optimized use of information technology.
  • Increased number of strategic collaborations with universities, law enforcement agencies, government institutions, the business sector, and professional organizations, as well as enhanced reputation of the Faculty of Law through excellent accreditation, faculty and student achievements, and recognition at the national and international levels.
